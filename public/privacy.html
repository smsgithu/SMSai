Privacy Policy

SMSai. Your AI Guide to Solana.

Introduction

SMSai ("we," "our," or "us"), at https://smsai.fun, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI chatbot.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • Wallet Address: When you connect your Solana wallet, we store your public wallet address to track XP and saved chats. We never have access to your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Sign-in: We ask your wallet to sign a message. That signature proves you control the wallet. It does not move funds.
  • Profile: If you set a display name or avatar color, we store that with your public wallet address so the leaderboard can show it.
  • Chat History: If you are signed in, we store your chat conversations so you can open them later.
  • Help tickets: If you use Get Help while Discord is connected, we send the topic, description, optional Discord username, and public wallet address to a Discord webhook. If help is not connected, we do not send the note.
  • Usage Data: We keep a running visit count. That count is not reset when leaderboard XP is reset.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Private keys, seed phrases, or passwords
  • Your legal name, email, or phone number
  • Financial information beyond public wallet addresses
  • Location data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered answers about Solana
  • To save your chat history (connected users only)
  • To track XP rewards and question counts
  • To improve our service

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Vercel's infrastructure. Chat histories are associated with wallet addresses, not personal identities.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Powers our AI responses
  • CoinGecko API: Provides cryptocurrency price data
  • Vercel: Hosts our application and database

Your Rights

You can:

  • Use the service without connecting a wallet (limited to 5 questions)
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time
  • Request deletion of your chat history by contacting us

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We never ask for or store private keys.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
