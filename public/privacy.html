Privacy Policy

smsai.fun - Your AI Guide to the Solana Ecosystem

Introduction

smsai.fun ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI chatbot service.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • Wallet Address: When you connect your Solana wallet, we store your public wallet address to track your usage and XP rewards. We never have access to your private keys or seed phrase.
  • Chat History: If you're a connected user, we store your chat conversations to provide chat history functionality.
  • Usage Data: We track anonymous visit counts and basic usage statistics.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Private keys or seed phrases
  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Financial information beyond public wallet addresses
  • Location data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered answers about Solana
  • To save your chat history (connected users only)
  • To track XP rewards and question counts
  • To improve our service

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Vercel's infrastructure. Chat histories are associated with wallet addresses, not personal identities.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Powers our AI responses
  • CoinGecko API: Provides cryptocurrency price data
  • Vercel: Hosts our application and database

Your Rights

You can:

  • Use the service without connecting a wallet (limited to 5 questions)
  • Disconnect your wallet at any time
  • Request deletion of your chat history by contacting us

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We never ask for or store private keys.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
